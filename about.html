About the programme

Closing the gap, one past paper at a time

CTSS Math Remedial is a free study resource for Grade 10 Mathematics and Grade 12 Advanced Mathematics students preparing for the National Exams — built and maintained by Mr. Larson Wii.

Why past papers?

Mathematics results at the National Exam level rarely come down to a lack of ability — they come down to exam craft: knowing the question styles, managing time across a paper, and having formulas ready without hesitation. The fastest way to build that craft is repetition on the real thing.

That's the whole idea behind this site: every paper here is an actual past CTSS exam paper, not a simplified imitation. Work through them under timed conditions, mark yourself honestly using the built-in tally on each paper, and revisit the topics that keep tripping you up.

How to use this site

Step 1

Pick your grade

Head to Grade 10 Mathematics or Grade 12 Advanced Mathematics and choose a year.

Step 2

Sit it like the real exam

Print or work from screen, under exam time limits, without your notes.

Step 3

Mark and tally

Enter your score on the paper's tracker — it's saved on your device so you can watch your trend over time.

Step 4

Review the gaps

Use the study notes to revisit any topic that cost you marks, then try another year.
