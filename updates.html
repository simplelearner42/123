22 June 2026

2026 National Exam Questions

Grade 10 Mathematics and Grade 12 Advanced Mathematics candidates sitting this year's National Exam should expect the usual split: a heavier weighting on algebra and functions early in the paper, followed by application questions that combine two or three topics at once.

The safest preparation is still the same one that works every year — sit as many past papers as you can under real time pressure, and mark them honestly. Use the tally on each paper's page on this site to see, at a glance, whether your score is trending up as exam day gets closer.

If your school has released a syllabus update for this year, cross-check it against the topics covered in the study notes and flag any gap to Mr. Wii via the contact page.

29 May 2026

Top 10 Exam Questions

Looking back across past Grade 10 Mathematics and Grade 12 Advanced Mathematics papers, a handful of question types reappear almost every year: solving simultaneous and quadratic equations, trigonometric ratios in right-angled and non-right-angled triangles, interpreting statistical data, and — for Advanced Mathematics — differentiation and integration questions built around real-world rates of change.

None of these are surprises. The students who do best are the ones who've drilled these exact question styles enough times that the method is automatic, leaving more time on exam day for the harder, less predictable questions.

Start with the most recent papers on the Grade 10 and Grade 12 A/Math pages, work backwards through the years, and keep a running tally of which of these ten question types still cost you marks.
